Our Story

A Passion for Culinary Excellence

Founded in 2018, EMBER & SAGE was born from a simple yet profound belief: that dining should be an experience that engages all the senses. Our name reflects the dual elements that define our cooking—the primal warmth of fire and the refined elegance of fresh herbs.

Every dish we create honors traditional techniques while embracing modern innovation. We source our ingredients from local farmers and artisans who share our commitment to quality and sustainability.

Learn More
Chef's Philosophy

Meet Chef Marcus Bellini

With over two decades of experience in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe and North America, Chef Marcus brings a unique perspective to modern cuisine. His philosophy is simple: respect the ingredient, understand the technique, and always cook with heart.

"Food is a universal language. At EMBER & SAGE, we speak it with passion, precision, and a deep respect for both tradition and innovation."
