Our Story

A journey of passion, precision, and the art of fine dining.

The Beginning

From a Dream to Reality

EMBER & SAGE was born in 2018 from the shared vision of Chef Marcus Bellini and restaurateur Elena Santos. After years of working in renowned kitchens across Europe and North America, they envisioned a restaurant that would honor traditional culinary craftsmanship while embracing contemporary innovation.

The name EMBER & SAGE reflects the dual elements that define their cooking philosophy—the primal warmth of fire and the refined elegance of fresh herbs. It's a place where rustic techniques meet sophisticated presentation, creating an experience that is both comforting and extraordinary.

Our Philosophy

Three pillars guide everything we do at EMBER & SAGE.

🔥

Fire & Flame

We believe in the transformative power of fire. From our wood-fired grill to our ember-roasted vegetables, flame is at the heart of our cooking, adding depth and character to every dish.

🌿

Fresh & Seasonal

We work closely with local farmers and artisan producers to source the finest seasonal ingredients. Our menu evolves with the seasons, ensuring peak flavor and freshness in every bite.

✨

Craft & Passion

Every dish is a labor of love. Our team brings dedication, precision, and creativity to the kitchen, transforming quality ingredients into culinary masterpieces that delight and inspire.

Meet the Chef

Chef Marcus Bellini

Executive Chef & Co-Founder

Born in Milan and trained in some of Europe's most prestigious kitchens, Chef Marcus brings over 20 years of culinary expertise to EMBER & SAGE. His journey has taken him from the Michelin-starred restaurants of Paris to the vibrant food scenes of New York and Tokyo.

His philosophy is rooted in respect—for the ingredients, for the techniques, and for the guests who entrust him with their dining experience. "Cooking is an act of generosity," he often says. "Every dish should tell a story and create a memory."

Under his leadership, EMBER & SAGE has earned recognition for its innovative approach to modern cuisine while maintaining the warmth and hospitality that makes guests feel at home.

Our Journey

Key moments that have shaped who we are today.

2018

The Beginning

EMBER & SAGE opens its doors in Portland's Pearl District, introducing a new concept in contemporary dining.

2019

First Accolades

Named "Best New Restaurant" by Portland Monthly and featured in Bon Appétit's list of America's best new restaurants.

2021

Expansion

The Garden Terrace opens, adding an outdoor dining experience and private event space to the restaurant.

2023

Recognition

Awarded our first James Beard nomination and recognized by the Oregon Restaurant & Lodging Association for Excellence.

2025

Innovation

Launch of our sustainable sourcing program, partnering directly with local farms for a farm-to-table experience.

Today

Continuing the Legacy

Continuing to push culinary boundaries while staying true to our roots of quality, passion, and hospitality.

Our Commitments

The values that guide our kitchen and our community.

Sustainability

Community
